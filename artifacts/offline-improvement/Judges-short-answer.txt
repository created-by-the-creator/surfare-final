We compared Surfare's local warnings with the original dataset labels across messages, links, QR codes and screenshot text. We measured correct results, false alarms and missed warnings, and compared the updated detector with its earlier version. It scored 95.5% on the original 200 cases and 92.25% on 400 additional cases, with 7.5% false positives and 8% false negatives on the latter. Because the sources overlap training or reference data, these are internal benchmark results, not independent real-world validation.
