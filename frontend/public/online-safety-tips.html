Online Safety Tips

Simple habits that help you slow down before you click, pay, or share.

Think before you click

  • Check the full web address before opening any link, especially one from an unsolicited message.
  • Type known websites directly or use official apps instead of following links sent to you.
  • Be careful with shortened links and QR codes from unknown sources.

Protect your accounts

  • Use a unique, long password for every account and keep them in a reputable password manager.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication wherever it is offered.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs, or recovery codes with anyone, including people who claim to be customer support.

Watch for pressure

Scammers often create urgency, fear, or excitement to rush your decision. When a message pressures you to act immediately, pause and verify through an independent, official channel.

Be careful with anyone who asks for money, favors, or personal details, even if they appear friendly or familiar. Accounts of friends and relatives can be hacked or cloned.

Shop and pay carefully

  • Prefer payment methods that offer buyer protection.
  • Avoid sending money directly to unknown individuals.
  • Research sellers, read independent reviews, and be careful with prices far below the market rate.

Keep your device in shape

  • Install system and app updates promptly.
  • Download apps only from official stores.
  • Avoid sensitive transactions on public Wi-Fi.

Protect your privacy

  • Limit personal information on public profiles and review privacy settings regularly.
  • Do not post photos of IDs, boarding passes, or bank cards.

If something goes wrong

Change affected passwords immediately, contact your bank or e-wallet provider, report the account or message to the platform, and keep screenshots as evidence. Acting quickly can help limit the damage.
