Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions we receive most often.

What is Surfare?

Surfare is a digital guide that checks messages, links, images, and QR codes for warning signs before you trust or act on them.

Is Surfare free to use?

Yes. The Safety Check is available at no cost.

Does Surfare prove something is safe?

No. Surfare points out warning signs and shows what an online check found. A clean result means no known threat found. It does not prove a site, message, or image is legitimate.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required to run a Safety Check.

What does the Online indicator mean?

It shows whether your device currently has an internet connection. Some checks need it. Local checks still work offline.

What happens when I check an image?

Reading text and QR codes stays on your device. The AI image check needs internet and sends a smaller copy with a few short file notes for visual review. The result gives clues, not proof.

What should I do if many warning signs are found?

Do not interact with the content. Do not click links, download files, or share information. Consider reporting it to the platform or relevant authorities.

A result seems wrong. What can I do?

No automated tool is perfect. Email contactsurfare@gmail.com with the details so we can review and improve.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email contactsurfare@gmail.com with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot.
