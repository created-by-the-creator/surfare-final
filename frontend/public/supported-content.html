Supported Content

The kinds of content Surfare is designed to review.

Web links (URLs)

Website addresses, shortened links, and links received through messages or email. We look for look-alike domains, suspicious structures, and common phishing patterns. When an online lookup is available, only the web address is checked. A clean lookup means no known threat found.

Messages and text

Text from SMS, chat applications, email, and social media posts or comments. We look for pressure tactics, impersonation, and requests for sensitive information.

Images

PNG, JPG, or WebP files up to 8 MB. You can read text in the image on your device, or use the online AI visual check. The AI check sends a smaller copy and a few short file notes. Its labels are clues, not proof.

QR codes

Upload a photo of a QR code or scan one with your camera. Decoded content is shown as text first. Links never open automatically.

Offers and listings

Online shopping offers, job postings, giveaways, and investment or earning opportunities that may be fraudulent.

Not currently supported

  • Private or login-protected pages that cannot be accessed.
  • Other files, attachments, and software or app installers.
  • Matters requiring legal, financial, or medical determination. Surfare is not a substitute for professional advice.

Future updates

Supported content types may expand over time. To request a content type, please write to contactsurfare@gmail.com.
