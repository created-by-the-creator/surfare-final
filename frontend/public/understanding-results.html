Understanding Results

How to read a Surfare check result.

What you will see

Every check gives you a short label, a plain-language summary, and any warning signs found. Read all three. One label does not tell the whole story.

The result labels

  • Calm Waters (Low risk): No strong warning signs were found in this check. If something still feels off, check again with an official source.
  • Choppy waves (Medium risk): Some warning signs were found. Do not click, reply, or pay until you confirm through an official channel.
  • Rip current (High risk): Several serious warning signs were found. Do not click links, download files, log in, or send money or personal details.
  • Foggy (Not enough info): There was too little to read. Try a clearer message, link, or photo.

Online link checks

When your device is online, only the web address is checked. A clean online result says no known threat found. It does not prove a site is legitimate. If the check is offline or unavailable, warning signs from the text still show.

Image checks

There are two choices. Read text in the image stays on your device. Check if the image looks AI-made needs internet and sends a smaller copy with a few short file notes for visual review.

  • Strong AI clues: several clues point toward AI making. This is still a clue, not proof.
  • Possible AI clues: some clues are present, but they can have other explanations.
  • No clear AI clues: nothing clear was found in this check.
  • Not sure: the image was too unclear, or the check did not finish.

Source notes in a file are clues too. They are not proof of where an image came from.

What Surfare cannot do

Surfare gives informational guidance from facts it can see. It does not prove that something is real, safe, or unsafe. New tricks may not be recognized yet. If something feels wrong, check with an official source.

Recommended next steps

  • Read the warning signs carefully.
  • Verify through the official website or hotline of the organization concerned.
  • Do not share personal or financial details while the matter is unverified.
  • Report suspected scams to the platform, your bank or e-wallet provider, or the proper authorities.
