About Surfare

Helping people take a careful second look online.

Our mission

To make everyday online checks simple and clear. Scams and misleading content can be hard to spot. Everyone deserves a plain-language guide before they trust.

What we do

Surfare checks content for warning signs and shows what is known from online checks. The result is written in plain language so you can decide what to do next.

Our principles

  • Clarity: results use plain words instead of technical jargon.
  • Responsibility: we are clear about what Surfare can and cannot determine.
  • Privacy: checks run on your device by default.
  • Education: every result is a chance to learn how to spot warning signs yourself.

Our commitment

We improve Surfare through community feedback and careful review. Your reports help us make the guide better.
